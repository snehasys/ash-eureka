CAB CITY

Cruise the city, pick up passengers waiting by the green beams, and drop them at the orange beam. Each shift runs 16:00 → midnight (8 minutes): hit the earnings target for a medal. Watch for passenger personalities, live events (closures, protests, rain, rush hour, theatres emptying) and chain near misses & drifts into style combos. Pick your city in Options — including a real map of Central London.
